Portfolios of Service.

Three ministerial portfolios spanning fiscal policy, environmental stewardship, and international trade — alongside a decade of economic advisory work and leadership across Kenya's institutional landscape.

December 2022 – Present

Principal Secretary
National Treasury

National Treasury Leadership & Team

Principal Secretary Dr. Chris Kiptoo alongside Cabinet Secretary Hon. John Mbadi, CBS, and senior members of the National Treasury team during strategic planning and policy review sessions at the Treasury Building, Nairobi.

Appointed by President William Samoei Ruto in December 2022, Dr. Kiptoo leads the National Treasury with a mandate centred on fiscal consolidation, debt sustainability, and structural reforms to accelerate Kenya's economic transformation under the Bottom-Up Economic Transformation Agenda.

FISCAL CONSOLIDATION

Reducing the budget deficit through disciplined expenditure and optimized resource allocation, targeting a 5.5% GDP fiscal deficit.

PUBLIC DEBT MANAGEMENT

Implementing a medium-term debt strategy to restructure obligations, extend maturities, and reduce borrowing costs for the Republic.

REVENUE MOBILIZATION

Coordinating with the Kenya Revenue Authority toward a KES 3.63 trillion domestic revenue target and modernization of the tax administration system.

STATE-OWNED ENTERPRISE REFORMS

Overseeing the rationalization and performance optimization of Kenya's public enterprises to reduce fiscal risk and enhance service delivery.

January 2020 – December 2022

Principal Secretary
Environment & Forestry

Serving as PS for the Ministry of Environment and Forestry from January 2020 to December 2022, Dr. Kiptoo led Kenya's national response to climate change, spearheaded ambitious reforestation programmes, and established the institutional framework for protecting Kenya's critical water towers.

CLIMATE CHANGE MITIGATION

Coordinating Kenya's Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs) under the Paris Agreement and building climate resilience frameworks across all government sectors.

KAPTAGAT CONSERVATION PROGRAMME

Founded the KICP to restore the Cherang'any and Elgeyo Hills ecosystems — reclaiming critical water towers through community partnership and multi-agency oversight.

FORESTRY DEVELOPMENT

Overseeing national tree-planting initiatives targeting 15 billion trees under Vision 2030, and managing sustainable forestry concessions across the country.

ENVIRONMENTAL POLICY COORDINATION

Aligning Kenya's environmental regulatory framework with global standards, including UNEP directives, and representing Kenya at international climate conferences.

January 2016 – January 2020

Principal Secretary
State Department of Trade

As PS for the State Department of Trade from January 2016 to January 2020, Dr. Kiptoo modernised Kenya's domestic commerce and international trading profile, championing regional integration, the AfCFTA framework, and logistics reform at Mombasa Port.

AFCFTA FRAMEWORK

Spearheaded Kenya's preparations for the African Continental Free Trade Area, building national capacity for tariff-free trade across the continent.

LOGISTICS & PORT MANAGEMENT

Overseeing efficiency reforms at Mombasa Port, achieving measurable reductions in cargo dwell times and regional transport costs.

EAC REGIONAL INTEGRATION

Led Kenya's delegation at East African Community negotiations in Arusha, reducing non-tariff barriers and harmonizing regional trade policies.

EMPRETEC & SME DEVELOPMENT

Established Kenya's first EMPRETEC Centre, a UN-backed entrepreneurship programme, to strengthen the capacity of small and medium enterprises.

2008 – 2012

Economic Advisory
Office of the Prime Minister

During Kenya's Grand Coalition Government under Prime Minister Raila Odinga, Dr. Kiptoo held three successive advisory roles coordinating economic policy at the highest level of the executive.

Senior Role

Economic Adviser to the Prime Minister

Provided direct macro-economic guidance to the Prime Minister's office, advising on national budget priorities, revenue policy, and Kenya's fiscal engagement with international financial institutions during the post-election reconciliation period.

Transitional Role

Deputy Economic Adviser

Supported the principal economic adviser in preparing policy briefs, coordinating inter-ministerial economic committees, and ensuring coherence in economic planning across the coalition government's diverse ministerial structure.

Initial Role

Director of Economic Policy Coordination

Directed the day-to-day coordination of economic policy formulation across government ministries, ensuring alignment with the Kenya Vision 2030 development blueprint and the Grand Coalition's economic stabilization agenda.
